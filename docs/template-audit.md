## Template and design findings

- The deck has 16 slides at 960 × 540 px (16:9). Preserve its organizer branding, top strip, footer, slide order, and editable text.
- Slide 1 is the branded AI Builder Cup cover with team-detail text. Slide 2 is a template-download instruction page; replace it with a concise executive summary. Slides 3–14 carry the submission topics above. Slide 15 is blank and can support evidence that does not fit another required field. Slide 16 is the branded “Thank you” ending.
- The body slides use a consistent white canvas, black header band with AI Builder Cup branding, restrained typography, and a thin footer rule. Use the template’s layout and typography, with one strong visual or diagram per slide where it clarifies the idea. Keep diagrams editable and use actual product screenshots for prototype evidence.
- Several source slides contain only section headings and little or no body content, so there is room to lay out concise, readable content without changing the visual system.
